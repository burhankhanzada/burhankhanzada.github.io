Maths note · the square root of a negative number

It looks like a question with no answer, and for a while everyone agreed it was. What number times itself gives −16-16−16? A positive times a positive is positive. A negative times a negative is also positive. Nothing on the number line works.

Nothing was approximated and nothing was skipped. The negative under the root did not stop the calculation — it changed what kind of answer came out, and that kind has a meaning: a pair, mirror images, whose real part sets how fast the response dies and whose imaginary part sets how fast it wobbles.
