From a picture of a machine to a controller you can defend

53 lessons in 9 parts: modelling, linearisation, Laplace, stability, and the controllers themselves. On every card you have to commit to an answer before the app will tell you anything — because the thing being trained here is the decision, not the algebra. Hints come one at a time, and any maths you are missing is one tap away, in the middle of the problem where you actually need it.

Every lesson is also a page of notes — the same material as continuous prose, with numbered equations and the questions as worked exercises. Read those first if you would rather see it explained before being asked; the link is on every tile below.
