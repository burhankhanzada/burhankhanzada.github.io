Appendix

The algebra the solutions skip

Every rearrangement the worked answers do in one jump — clearing fractions, a fraction over a fraction, splitting one into several, what you may cross out and what you may not, matching coefficients, and taking a limit without calculus.

Two moves, and everything else is bookkeeping

Every rearrangement in this course is one of two things: doing the same thing to both sides, and collecting the unknown onto one side so it can be factored out. Nothing more sophisticated appears anywhere, and the derivations that look like a single leap are always four or five of these in a row with the middle ones unwritten.

Here is the leap the whole s-domain half of the course rests on, with every line of it on the page. Start from what the loop says:

Crossing out: what you may cancel, and what you may not

One rule, and it is worth reading twice because almost every piece of wrong algebra in this subject is a violation of it:

You may cancel a factor of the whole top against a factor of the whole bottom. You may never cancel something that is only one term of a sum.

The test to apply before crossing anything out: can I write the top as a product with this thing as one of its factors, and the bottom as a product with this thing as one of its factors? If yes, cancel. If either side is a sum with the thing buried in one term of it, stop.
