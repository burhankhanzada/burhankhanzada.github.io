This is the standard move for turning any high-order equation into a list of first-order ones: name each derivative as the next state, and the last equation carries the physics.

It always follows the same skeleton — a chain of "this one is the next one’s rate", finished by one real equation. Every exam question that asks for state-space form is asking for exactly this.
